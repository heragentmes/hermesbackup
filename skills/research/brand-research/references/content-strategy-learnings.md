# Content Strategy Learnings (from RockSit Project)

## Key User Corrections

### 1. Content must sound human-written, not AI-generated
The user explicitly rejected content that sounded like it was written by AI. This is a hard requirement.

**Bad (AI-sounding):**
```
شرکت ما با بهره‌گیری از تکنولوژی‌های نوین و نیروی انسانی متخصص، 
آماده ارائه خدمات مهندسی در حوزه ساخت و ساز می‌باشد.
```

**Good (human-sounding):**
```
ما تو راکسیت یه تیم ۱۵ نفره‌ایم که عاشق ساختمون ساختنیم.
هر پروژه‌ای که تحویل میدیم، انگار بچه خودمونه.
```

### 2. Content must be segmented by platform
Never publish the same content everywhere. Each platform has different:
- Audience (general vs. professional vs. B2B)
- Tone (casual vs. expert vs. formal)
- Content type (visual vs. analytical vs. technical)

### 3. LinkedIn company page is a priority
When a brand only has a CEO personal page on LinkedIn, the company page must be created first. This is often overlooked.

## Platform-Specific Content Rules

### Instagram (@rocksit.co)
- Visual posts with project photos
- Short reels (30-60 seconds)
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Casual, friendly tone
- 4-5 posts per week

### Telegram (@toartin + @rockcity_ir)
- Exclusive analysis from CEO
- News summaries with personal opinion
- Project details with specifics
- Expert, opinionated tone
- 2-3 posts per week

### LinkedIn (Company page + CEO profile)
- Long-form articles
- Market analysis
- Company achievements
- Team introductions
- Professional, formal tone
- 2 posts per week

### Website (rocksit.ir)
- Complete company information
- Project details
- Blog articles
- Contact forms
- Official, comprehensive tone
- 1 article per week

## Natural Tone Examples

### ❌ Bad (AI-sounding):
- "شرکت ما با بهره‌گیری از تکنولوژی‌های نوین..."
- "ما مفتخریم که خدمات با کیفیت ارائه می‌دهیم"
- "این یک دستاورد بزرگ برای ماست"

### ✅ Good (human-sounding):
- "دیروز رفتیم سر پروژه زعفرانیه..."
- "ما تو راکسیت یه تیم ۱۵ نفره‌ایم..."
- "حقیقتش رو بخوای، این پروژه یکی از سخت‌ترین‌ها بود..."
- "بچه‌ها واقعاً پرتلاشن..."
